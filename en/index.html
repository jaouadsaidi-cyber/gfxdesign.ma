Our expertise

Every industry, turnkey solutions

From choosing the camera to the production report, we handle the entire inspection chain. You gain a partner who masters optics, software and automation alike.

01

Machine vision integration

We select the sensor, optics and lighting, develop the inspection and install it on your line, through to commissioning.

  • Identification: 1D and 2D codes, character reading and verification (OCR, OCV)
  • Surface inspection: defects, colour, presence or absence
  • Non-contact dimensional measurement
  • Assembly verification and robot guidance
  • 3D and profilometry
02

Vision software

Our cross-platform software suite runs the inspection, guides the operator and keeps a record of every part.

  • Clear interface, guided setup and built-in help
  • GPU-accelerated artificial intelligence
  • Results archived locally, on a server or in the cloud
  • Production reports and statistics
  • Data exchange with your PLCs and databases
03

Automation and Industry 4.0

We connect vision to the rest of the plant: test benches, automatic sorting, traceability and data feedback to your systems.

  • Custom test benches and inspection stations
  • Automatic sorting, rejection and classification
  • Integration on machines, conveyors and robots
  • Part and batch traceability
  • Industrial communication: digital I/O, serial, CAN, Ethernet, Profinet, Modbus, OPC
04

Training

Hands-on training to make your teams self-sufficient with their vision and automation systems.

  • Vision fundamentals: sensor, optics, lighting, image processing
  • Cognex In-Sight and VisionPro
  • Industrial automation
  • On a demonstration bench or directly on your equipment
  • Programmes tailored to your quality, engineering and maintenance teams

Applications

What our systems inspect

    Identification

  • 04

    Checking the presence, position and orientation of components.

  • 05

    Locating the part after camera calibration, to guide the robot.

Method

From idea to production line, in four steps

  1. 01

    Feasibility study

    We test the inspection on your actual parts, good and defective, before sizing the solution.

  2. 02

    Design

    Sensor, optics, lighting and processing unit are chosen to match your line speed, target accuracy and budget.

  3. 03

    Integration and commissioning

    Installation on the line, connection to the automation, fine-tuning under real production conditions.

  4. 04

    Training and support

    Handover to your teams, assistance, maintenance and system upgrades.
